Planned Towing, Not Just Emergencies

Not every job is a roadside emergency. A lot of what we do is planned: a car that won't start needs moving from a driveway to a garage, a non-runner bought privately needs collecting, or a vehicle needs transporting between two locations without being driven.

We use a flatbed recovery vehicle, so your car or van is loaded and carried rather than towed on its wheels — safer for the vehicle and legal for cars that can't be driven at all, including ones with no MOT (transport to a pre-booked destination like a garage or auction site is generally permitted even without a current MOT — check guides.html for the full detail).

Whether it's a single car moving across Leicester or a vehicle coming in from further afield, we quote a clear price upfront before we load anything.
