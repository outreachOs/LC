🚨 Broken Down at M1 Junction 20 or on the A-Roads — Do This First

1. On the M1, leave at the next junction or reach an emergency area if you can
2. On an A-road, get as far onto the left verge or into a lay-by as possible
3. Hazard lights on as you slow down
4. Leave the vehicle from the side away from traffic, when it is safe
5. Stand well back, behind a barrier where there is one
6. If you are in a live lane or anyone is in danger, call 999 first
7. Note your direction of travel and the nearest junction or marker post
8. Once you are safe, call Kris Recovery on 07865 449983
