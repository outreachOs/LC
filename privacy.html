Privacy & Cookie Policy

How Leicester Car Recovery uses enquiry information and website measurement data.

Last updated: 18 October 2026. Leicester Car Recovery provides vehicle recovery services in Leicester and Leicestershire. If you have a privacy question or request, call 07865 449983.

Information we collect

When you call, request a callback, book a job, use the contact form or send a website-chat message, we may receive your name, phone number, vehicle and journey details, and the content of your enquiry. We also receive limited technical and usage data from our website measurement tools, such as page visits and clicks on call or WhatsApp buttons.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to you, provide or arrange recovery services, maintain service and security, understand which advertising brings genuine enquiries, and improve the website. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and advertising measurement

The site uses Google Tag Manager and Microsoft Advertising Universal Event Tracking (UET). Microsoft UET helps us measure visits and actions such as clicking the phone number, WhatsApp or successfully sending an enquiry. Advertising storage is denied by default and is changed only if you select “Allow” in the website banner. Your choice is stored in your browser. Learn more in the Microsoft Privacy Statement and Google Privacy Policy.

Service providers and retention

We use service providers to host the website, deliver enquiry notifications and measure advertising. They process information only for those services and under their own applicable terms. We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the enquiry, provide the service, meet legal obligations and resolve disputes.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data-protection law may give you rights to ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection, and to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office. To make a request, call us using the number above.
