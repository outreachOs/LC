🚨 Broken Down on the M1 or A-Roads Near the Airport — Do This First

1. On the M1, leave at the next junction or reach a service or emergency area if you can
2. On the A453, A50 or A42, get onto the left verge or into a lay-by
3. Hazard lights on as you slow down
4. Leave the vehicle from the side away from traffic, when it is safe
5. Stand well back from the carriageway, behind a barrier where there is one
6. If you are in a live lane or anyone is in danger, call 999 first
7. Note your direction of travel and the nearest junction, marker post or car-park zone
8. Once you are safe, call Kris Recovery on 07865 449983

A van or light commercial that won't start on an East Midlands Gateway unit or the A50 freight route. We bring the right equipment — see van recovery.

Donington Park event day

Flat batteries and non-starts in the Donington Park car parks and on the approach roads after a race or festival. Pre-book if you know you'll need a lift home.

A6 Kegworth breakdown

Clutch, gearbox or overheating faults on the A6 Kegworth bypass. If it can't be driven, we load it and take it to your garage.
