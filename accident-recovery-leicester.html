Accident Recovery

Calm, Careful Recovery After a Collision

An accident is stressful enough without worrying about how the car gets moved. If your vehicle isn't driveable — or you simply don't feel safe driving it — we recover it directly from the scene, including from live carriageways and motorway hard shoulders where it's safe to do so.

We're used to working alongside the other people involved in an accident recovery: police at the scene where attended, your insurer's claims line, and the bodyshop or approved repairer the vehicle needs to go to. Tell us the destination and we'll take it straight there rather than to a holding yard, where that's possible.

If you're unsure who's paying for recovery — you, the other driver's insurer, or your own policy's roadside cover — we can still recover the vehicle now and you can sort that out afterwards. Getting the car off the road safely comes first.

Accident Recovery

Accident-Damaged and Non-Drivable Vehicle Recovery

A vehicle does not need to be completely immobilised to require professional recovery. After an accident, damaged steering, suspension, wheels, tyres, bodywork or leaking fluids can make a car or van unsafe to drive.

We recover accident-damaged and non-drivable vehicles throughout Leicester and Leicestershire, including vehicles involved in roadside collisions, low-speed impacts and incidents away from home. The vehicle can be transported to a local garage, repair centre, bodyshop, storage location or another suitable destination requested by the customer, using our vehicle transport service.

If the vehicle is in a dangerous position or anyone is injured, customers should contact the emergency services first. Once the scene is safe and recovery is permitted, they can call Kris Recovery for vehicle collection and transport.
