🚨 M1 Motorway Safety — Do This First

1. Leave the motorway at the next junction or reach a service area if possible
2. Otherwise, use an emergency area or hard shoulder where available
3. Switch on your hazard lights
4. Leave the vehicle from the side furthest from traffic, when it's safe to do so
5. Wait behind a safety barrier where possible and safe
6. If stopped in a live lane, unable to exit safely, or anyone is in immediate danger — call 999
7. In an emergency area, use the emergency telephone and follow the operator's instructions
8. Once the situation is safe, call Kris Recovery for vehicle recovery
