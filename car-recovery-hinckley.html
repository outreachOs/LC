Hinckley is boxed in by fast roads. Hinckley Breakdowns

Common Breakdown Situations Around Hinckley

Blowout on the A5

Tyre failures at speed on the A5 Watling Street near Dodwells and Longshoot. Get onto the verge, hazards on, and call — we carry recovery equipment for cars and vans.

M69 Junction 1

Power loss or warning lights on the M69 slip roads linking the A5 and the M1. Reach an emergency area, stay behind the barrier, then call for motorway recovery.

Van down on an estate

Commercial vehicles stuck at Tungsten Park, Harrowbrook or Sketchley Meadows. We recover vans and light commercials so the job isn't lost — see van recovery.

Flat battery in town

Non-starts in the town-centre car parks, around The Crescent and Britannia. We can jump-start where possible or recover the car to a garage or home.

Clutch on Rugby Road

Clutch and gearbox failures on the B4109 Rugby Road and the A47 towards Earl Shilton. If it can't be driven, we load it and take it to your garage.

Cross-border transport

Moving a non-runner or an accident-damaged car between Hinckley and Nuneaton, Bedworth or Coventry. Pre-book a slot and we'll quote the drop up front.
