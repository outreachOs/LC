🚨 Broken Down on the A46 — Do This First

1. If the car still drives, leave at the next junction or pull into a lay-by
2. Otherwise get as far onto the left verge as possible
3. Hazard lights on as you slow down
4. Leave the vehicle from the side away from traffic, when it is safe
5. Stand well back from the carriageway, behind a barrier where there is one
6. If you are stopped in a live lane or anyone is in danger, call 999 first
7. Note the nearest junction number, slip road or landmark
8. Once you are safe, call Kris Recovery on 07865 449983

The Bypass That Rings Leicester

The A46 is the high-speed dual carriageway that carries traffic around the west and north of Leicester, linking the M1 at Junction 21A with the routes towards Newark and the A1. It is grade-separated with fast-moving traffic and long gaps between junctions, so a breakdown here needs a recovery operator who knows exactly where you are and the quickest safe way to reach you.

We run this corridor daily — the A50 Groby Road junction for Glenfield and Groby, the A6 Loughborough Road junction at Birstall and Rothley, and the A607 Melton Road junction at Syston. Breakdowns on the A46 are typically fast failures: tyre blowouts, loss of power, overheating in queues at the busier roundabouts, and vehicles running low on fuel between junctions.

Call Kris directly and you speak to the driver who turns up. You will get a realistic arrival time for your exact position on the A46 and a clear price before anything moves.
